## App name (30 тэмдэгт хүртэл)
Rune Rivals: Card Battle

## Short description (80 тэмдэгт хүртэл)
Draft cards, grow your army and outlast your rival in auto-battle lane duels!

## Full description
Rune Rivals is a pixel-art strategy duel. You and your rival each defend your own lane against endless waves of monsters. Your hero and soldiers fight on their own. Your job is to pick the right cards between waves.

HOW IT WORKS
• Every turn you get energy and 3 cards to choose from
• Summon soldiers, elites and even a Champion
• Boost your army, heal your hero, set traps and frost runes
• Sabotage your rival: send goblins, bats or a hired ogre into their lane
• Waves get tougher every turn. The last hero standing wins!

FEATURES
• 30 cards: troops, boosts, hero powers, sabotage, economy and tactics
• 10 monster types, from slimes that split to the mighty Ember Drake
• 4 heroes with unique attacks: Arin, Brakka, Seren and Volka
• 6 ranked leagues from Bronze to Mythic, with rewards and new cards
• Choose starting cards to shape your strategy
• Level up your heroes and train your army
• Handmade pixel art and chiptune music
• Short matches, perfect for a quick break. Plays offline.

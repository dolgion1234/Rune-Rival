Rune Rivals – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Rune Rivals ("the game") is developed by Rune Rivals Studio. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

The game does not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your email, location or contacts. Your progress (player name you choose, trophies, gold, gems, heroes, cards and settings) is saved only on your device. Opponents in the game are computer-controlled; no data is shared with other players.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to those transactions: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The game may show ads, including optional reward videos, provided by Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners. These services may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit the Advertising ID in your device settings. See the Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Third-party services

The game is built with Unity. Unity may collect anonymous technical data (such as device model and crash information) as described in the Unity Privacy Policy linked above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
